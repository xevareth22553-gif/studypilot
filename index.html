15 AI Study Prompts

Use AI as a study partner instead of simply asking it for answers.

01Explain [topic] simply, then give me 5 questions.
02Teach me [topic] step-by-step and check my understanding.
03Quiz me on [topic] one question at a time.
04Create 10 practice questions from easy to difficult.
05Create a short exam-style test and grade it after I finish.
06Review my answer and tell me what I got right and what I missed.
07Compare [A] and [B] in a simple table.
08Give me a real-world example of [concept].
09Turn my notes into active-recall questions.
10Turn this chapter into a one-page revision sheet.
11Separate these notes into must-know, useful, and optional.
12Make me a 7-day study plan.
13Create a mnemonic for [concept].
14Act as a strict but helpful examiner.
15Prioritize what I should study for my exam.
